Research Statement

I am interested in understanding how humans and machines "think" differently. For this reason, my work focuses on tasks where AI systems fail to exhibit human-like behavior and reasoning patterns. I believe reducing these gaps would allow humans and AI systems to collaborate more effectively. Besides this, I am also interested in AI for education and AI safety.
